# Task 6 – Importance of Patch Management

## Objective

The objective of this task is to understand the importance of patch management in cybersecurity, study real-world incidents caused by unpatched vulnerabilities, and identify effective patch management practices.

## Topics Covered

- Software vulnerabilities
- CVEs and vulnerability exploitation
- WannaCry and EternalBlue
- Equifax data breach
- Patch management lifecycle
- Risk-based prioritization
- Patch management challenges and solutions
- Security best practices

## Report Contents

The report covers:

- Definition and importance of patch management
- Vulnerabilities, CVEs, and exploitation
- Two real-world security incidents
- Consequences of poor patch management
- Patch management lifecycle
- Seven-step patch management checklist
- Common challenges and possible solutions
- Patch management best practices

## Deliverable

`patch_management_report.md`

## Resources Used

- NIST cybersecurity publications
- CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog
- CVE Program

## Ethical Considerations

This task is a research-based cybersecurity activity. No unauthorized exploitation, scanning, or attacks were performed against real systems or organizations.

## Conclusion

This report demonstrates the importance of maintaining updated systems and following a structured patch management process to reduce cybersecurity risks.
