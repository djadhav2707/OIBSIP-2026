# Task 5 – Social Engineering Attacks

## Objective

The objective of this task is to study common social engineering attacks, understand how attackers manipulate victims, and identify suitable prevention techniques.

## Attacks Covered

- Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Whaling
- Vishing
- Smishing
- Pretexting
- Baiting
- Quid Pro Quo

## Report Contents

The report covers:

- How social engineering attacks work
- Types of phishing attacks
- Real-world case studies
- Psychological techniques used by attackers
- Impact of social engineering attacks
- Prevention and mitigation techniques
- Comparison of different attack types
- Employee security-awareness checklist

## Deliverable

`social_engineering_report.md`

## Resources Used

- CISA cybersecurity guidance
- NIST publications
- SANS security resources
- FBI cybersecurity guidance

## Ethical Considerations

This task is a research-based cybersecurity activity. No social engineering attack, phishing campaign, credential collection, or unauthorized activity was performed against real users or organizations.

## Conclusion

This report demonstrates an understanding of common social engineering techniques and the security-awareness practices that can help organizations protect employees and sensitive information.
